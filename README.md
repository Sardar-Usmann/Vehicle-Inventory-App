# Real-Time Vehicle Parts Inventory & Billing System

A modern **Flutter and Firebase-based vehicle parts management application** designed to streamline inventory management, billing, customer orders, and delivery tracking.

The application combines real-time cloud synchronization, role-based access, QR-based part identification, and delivery monitoring to help vehicle parts businesses manage their daily operations efficiently.

## Features

### Authentication and User Management
- User registration and login
- Customer and staff roles
- Firebase Authentication integration
- Role-based application access

### Inventory Management
- Add, update, and delete vehicle parts
- Manage product quantities and pricing
- Search and filter inventory
- Low-stock monitoring
- Real-time inventory synchronization

### Billing and Sales
- QR code-based part identification
- Shopping cart management
- Automatic bill calculation
- Sales record management
- Transaction history

### Orders and Customer Management
- Browse available vehicle parts
- Place and manage orders
- Track order status
- View order history
- Customer-specific order access

### Delivery Tracking
- Delivery status management
- Map-based delivery visualization
- Staff and destination location tracking
- Distance and estimated arrival time support

### Staff Operations
- Manage vehicle parts inventory
- Process customer orders
- Generate bills
- Update delivery status
- Monitor sales activities
